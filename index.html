Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Contact: privacy@noctis.ai

1. Introduction

At Noctis AI, we believe family privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy clearly describes what data we collect, why we collect it, how third-party services assist us in delivering the app, and how you retain total control over your and your family's information.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data that is strictly necessary to authenticate you, create your personalized stories, and manage your subscription.

A. Account & Authentication Data

  • Email Address & One-Time Password (OTP): When you sign in with your email, we send a 6-digit OTP code to verify your identity. We do not use or store permanent account passwords. The OTP code is verified by Supabase Auth and discarded immediately.
  • Sign in with Apple: If you choose Apple authentication, we receive an Apple user identifier, an identity token, and (only during your very first sign-in) the email address and full name you authorize Apple to share.
  • Sign in with Google: If you choose Google authentication, we receive Google OAuth identity and access tokens to authenticate your account.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure session tokens (access and refresh tokens) are stored locally on your device in secure storage (AsyncStorage / SecureStore) to keep you signed in.

B. Child & Story Preference Data

To generate customized stories for your child, you provide:

  • Child's Name: Stored in your profile and used in story text and illustrations.
  • Child's Age: (Ages 1 to 12) Used to adjust reading complexity and vocabulary.
  • Preferred Language: Stored in your profile to generate stories and narration in your selected language.
  • Story Preferences: Themes, morals or lessons, and story settings you select during story creation.
  • Favorites: Stories you flag as favorites in your library.

C. Generated Story Content & Media Storage

  • Story Text & Titles: Generated stories and their titles are stored in our secure database linked to your user account so you can read them at any time.
  • Illustration Images: Cover images generated for your stories are stored in our secure cloud storage bucket (story-images).
  • Narration Audio (TTS): Text-to-speech narration files are stored in our secure cloud storage bucket (story-audio) so you can listen to them on demand.
  • Generation Logs: We record timestamps and generation types (story, image, speech) to enforce daily and plan-specific limits.

D. Subscription & Payment Data

All financial transactions are handled exclusively by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Noctis AI never collects, receives, or stores your credit card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses. We receive only transaction metadata via RevenueCat (such as whether your subscription is active, plan type, expiration date, and store receipt identifiers).

E. Technical & Device Information Collected by SDKs

When you use the app, the RevenueCat SDK automatically collects limited device data to validate purchases and deliver updates:

  • Device model, operating system (iOS/Android), OS version, app version, and device locale/language.
  • IP address (used to determine store region/currency and prevent fraudulent activity).
  • Advertising Identifiers (IDFA on iOS or GAID on Android): Only collected if you explicitly grant permission via your device settings. We do not run advertisements.
[FLAG for Developer Review]: If you do not intend to track advertising campaigns, ensure that you disable IDFA collection in your RevenueCat SDK configuration to avoid requiring Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Providing the Core Service: Generating personalized story text, illustrations, and audio narrations tailored to your child's age and language.
  • Personal Library: Preserving your created stories, illustrations, and audio so you can revisit them across your devices.

The safety of children is our highest priority. Noctis AI complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K) in the European Union and United Kingdom.

  • No Child Direct Accounts: Children are not permitted to register accounts. Accounts may only be created by adults (parents, legal guardians, or authorized adult caregivers with explicit parental permission).
  • Minimal Data Collection: We only collect the child's first name, age (1–12), and preferred language to personalize the bedtime stories. We do not collect child email addresses, physical addresses, biometric identifiers, or school information.
  • Parental Rights: Parents have the right at any time to:
    1. Review the stories and profile information saved in the app;
    2. Edit or update their child's name, age, or language in the app Settings;
    3. Delete individual stories; or
    4. Permanently erase all child information and the entire user account via the in-app delete button.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information directly from a child without verified parental consent, please contact us immediately at privacy@noctis.ai, and we will promptly delete such records.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Retention Period: We retain your profile data, stories, illustration images, and audio files for as long as your account remains active, so you can enjoy your library.

Instant Self-Serve Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly inside the app:

  1. Open the Noctis AI app.
  2. Navigate to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap "Delete Account" and confirm your choice.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account: Our system triggers an automated database cascade (via a secure Supabase Edge Function) that immediately and permanently deletes:

  • Your user authentication record and email address;
  • Your profile and child details;
  • All saved stories and their text;
  • All story cover images in the story-images cloud storage bucket;
  • All story audio files in the story-audio cloud storage bucket;
  • All generation logs and subscription database records.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Account deletion does not automatically cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription; you must cancel active subscriptions through your device store settings as explained in Section 4 of our Terms.

7. Data Security

We employ robust technical safeguards to protect your family's data:

  • All communications between the mobile app, backend servers, and AI APIs are encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.
  • All database tables enforce Supabase Row Level Security (RLS), meaning users can strictly and only read or write their own data.
  • Media files in cloud storage are organized under user-specific paths and protected against unauthorized public access.
