Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, name (if provided during sign-up), and account credentials (passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plaintext).

Child Information: When you create stories, you may provide child's name, age, gender, appearance, and additional details for story personalization.

Story Content: The stories you generate are stored in your personal library within our secure database. This allows you to access, read, and manage your stories across devices.

Authentication Data: When you sign in with Apple, we receive authentication tokens and basic profile information (email and name, if provided) from Apple.

Quota Data: We track your story generation quota to enforce plan limits (free vs. Ultra plan).

How We Use Information

Providing App Functionality: We use your information to provide, maintain, and improve our story generation services.

Account Management: Your account information is used to manage your account, authenticate your access, and provide personalized experiences.

Story Generation: Child information is used to generate personalized stories via Google's Gemini API.

Customer Support: We may use your information to respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.

Security: We use your information to detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security threats.

Data Sharing

We share data with the following third-party services to operate our app:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication services
  • Apple: Authentication services (when you sign in with Apple). Apple provides your email and name (on first authorization only) according to their privacy policy.
  • Google Gemini API: When you generate stories, your prompts and story content are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate story text, cover illustrations, and audio narration (text-to-speech). Your inputs are subject to Google's own privacy policy and API data-use terms.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription and purchase management services, to process and validate your in-app purchases.
  • Apple App Store: For in-app purchase processing (subscriptions)

Note: Google Play Store integration is planned for future Android builds but not currently implemented.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy.

Account Data: Retained until you delete your account.

Story Data: Retained until you delete the story or your account. Stories are not automatically deleted after a period of inactivity.

Audio Caching: Generated audio files are cached temporarily on-device and deleted immediately after playback or on app unmount. These temporary files are not stored in our database or linked to your account in any persistent manner.

Account Deletion: When you delete your account, your personal information and account data are deleted immediately, except where required by law.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

All data is transmitted using secure encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and stored in secure cloud infrastructure.
